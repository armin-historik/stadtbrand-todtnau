ORIGINAL EXCERPT · SOURCE VERIFICATION

What the newspaper actually reported

The excerpt from the Schwäbische Kronik, issue 172 of 22 July 1876, documents the immediate consequences of the town fire.

What can be read in the excerpt

  • Location: Todtnau; date of the fire: 19 July 1876
  • The fire started at Ziegler's paper mill
  • 88 houses burned down
  • 202 families and approximately 1,200 people left homeless
  • References to relief efforts and factories spared by the fire

Interpreting the source

The time of 7 p.m. given in the archive record cannot be verified as the start of the fire from the newspaper excerpt shown. Hanser places the outbreak at around noon.

Source: Schwäbische Kronik, no. 172, 22 July 1876, archive HG-SBT-Z-0005.

Notes on source criticism

Archive catalogue records are research aids. Where a summary differs from the original text, the original takes precedence. The near-contemporary account by Pastor Scherer will be compared separately with its original scan before any comparison of times is made.
