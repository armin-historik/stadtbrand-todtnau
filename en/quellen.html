Methodology

Which sources do we use?

Contemporary evidence

Reports and administrative records dating from the time of the fire, including the Schwäbische Kronik of 22 July 1876 and Pastor Scherer's notes.

Historical visual sources

Drawings of the old townscape and photographs of the destruction. Dates, depicted subjects, provenance and usage rights are checked separately.

Later accounts

Retrospective accounts of local history, including the Todtnauer Nachrichten of 25 July 2025, are compared with earlier sources.

Technical comparative literature

Publications on fire protection, wooden shingle roofs and building insurance explain possible connections. They are not direct evidence of conditions in Todtnau in 1876.

Further identified archival holdings

The following titles are listed in publicly accessible finding aids. A catalogue entry establishes the existence and description of a file, not its precise contents.

  • Freiburg State Archive, B 719/1 no. 1560: Alteration of the main road within Todtnau during reconstruction, 1876–1879, five sheets. Catalogue entry.
  • Stockach Town Archive, II.2/2: Collection for victims of the Todtnau fire; accountability report of the relief committee, 1876–1877. Collection inventory (PDF).
  • LEO-BW Historical Gazetteer: Description of the grid-pattern reconstruction and the new parish church built in 1878–1881. Local history entry.

Fire brigades and firefighting: new references

  • Municipality of Wembach: History of the Wembach fire brigade, including details of its deployment on 19/20 July 1876 and a historical catering invoice. View source.
  • Town of Todtnau: Local historical account of the fire, including flying embers, wind, participating firefighters and 23 pumps. View source.
  • Todtnau Town Band: History of the association mentioning the loss of much of the fire brigade band's records in the town fire. View source.

Source status: publicly accessible later accounts; wherever possible, their original evidence must be examined separately for historical reconstruction.
